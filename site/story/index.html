01

1999

Amairo, Teso South, Busia

Born in Amairo

Son of Titus Etyang and Janeros Orodi. Brother to Davis, Mercy, Felix and Polycarp.

Thadeus Esirom Etyang was born on 7 July 1999 in Amairo, Teso South, to Titus Etyang and Janeros Orodi.

  • FamilyBrother to Davis, Mercy, Felix and Polycarp. To the family he was simply Tedy.
  • HomeAmairo is where it all started, and where his heart stayed. Years later, every big dream he had was for this village.

He was a bubbly, playful boy, the life of every gathering, and never short of friends. Many of the friendships he made as a child stayed with him for life.

02

2005–2008

Okook

Okook Primary

Where school began.

Okook Primary was where little Tedy first carried a school bag.

Break time was his favourite subject: sand, mud, football with whatever ball could be found, and a crew of friends always around him.

Most evenings he came home with mud on his uniform and a big grin, like a real legend.

In class 4, Mum and Dad moved him to Isegere Toto, a private school, to give his studies a stronger push.

2009–2013

Isegere

Isegere Toto School

Not the top of the class, never short on ambition.

Books were never his strongest game. His report cards did not always say much, but they never dented his ambition an inch. He always knew he was going somewhere.

  • FootballHe made the school's first team, wearing the No. 6 shirt.
  • FarmingA proud member of the 4K Club. Spent time in the school's greenhouse. His love for the land started here.

He graduated from primary school in November 2013, gown on, smile wide.

04

2014–2017

Nambale

Nambale High School

Four years of high school.

In 2014 Tedy joined Nambale High School, and the playful boy from Amairo began growing into a young man.

He made friends the way he always did: fast, and for life. Wherever there was laughter in the dorm or noise on the pitch, he was usually close by.

  • FootballStill on the pitch whenever he could be, and a loyal Manchester United fan through every high and low.

He sat his KCSE in 2017 and closed the school chapter, ready to try his luck in the big city.

05

2018–2019

Nairobi

The Nairobi hustle

Two years finding his way in the city.

After school, Tedy headed to Nairobi to find his way.

He enrolled at AA Driving School and got behind the wheel. He took on driving jobs, worked at a hardware store, and said yes to whatever honest work came his way.

It was a season of trying things out, of long days and figuring himself out. He never stopped moving, and he never lost the smile.

By 2020 he had found his direction: machines.

06

2019–2020

Sensei Institute

Trained as a Plant Operator.

In 2019 Tedy enrolled at Sensei Institute for a short course in plant operation: excavators, loaders and the heavy machines that build roads and cities.

He took to it quickly. For a young man who loved being behind the wheel, this was the next level.

He then interned with the County Government of Busia, back home, where he sharpened the skill on real machines and real roads.

Then, in early 2020, Covid hit and the world stood still. Plans paused, but Tedy was already thinking about his next move.

07

2021

RTS Eldoret

Kenya Army

Recruit training began in 2021. He wore the uniform with pride.

In 2021 Tedy was recruited into the Kenya Defence Forces, following in his father's footsteps. Titus had served too, and now his son wore the same uniform. It was a proud day for the whole family back in Amairo.

Recruit training was tough: early mornings, long drills, and pushing past limits he did not know he had.

He came out of it a soldier, standing taller, but still with the same easy laugh and the same "Niaje bro" for everyone.

10

His dream

Building for Home

Everything he dreamed of was for the village, and for Teso.

Tedy never forgot where he came from. Wherever life took him, his biggest plans were for Amairo and the people back home. These were the dreams he talked about, and the ones he was working towards.

  • LandHe loved farming and believed in food security. He wanted to farm on a scale that would help keep food on the table for families around him.
  • WaterHe planned to set up a free water point in the village, so no one would have to walk far for clean water.
  • WorkMost of his ideas were about jobs. He wanted to create work close to home, so young people would not have to leave the village to earn a living.
  • VoiceHe felt Teso culture and the Ateso language were too little known. He dreamed of a free recording studio where young people could make music in Ateso and carry their culture to the world.
  • FaithHis faith was the root of it all. He believed he was meant to help people, and he chose to start with home.

He did not get the time to build them. But the people who loved him know exactly what he wanted, and that is where his story continues.

11

2026

The Campfire

In March 2026 he fell ill with a rare blood cell disease.

In March 2026, Tedy fell ill.

For months, no one could say why. Test after test came back negative, while his blood counts kept falling and, in time, began to affect his organs. Through it all he went in and out of hospital, discharged three times and back again, and he came through three surgeries.

In October, the answer finally came: Paroxysmal Nocturnal Hemoglobinuria (PNH), a rare condition of the blood. A fourth and final surgery was set for November.

On the evening of Saturday, 7 November, he was taken into intensive care. On the evening of Wednesday, 11 November 2026, Tedy breathed his last.

He was only 27.

Unfinished dreams. The end of an era.

But the fire he lit in all of us still burns.
